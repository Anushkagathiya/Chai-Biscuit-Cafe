About Us

Welcome to Chai Biscuit Cafe! We are passionate about creating the perfect atmosphere for you to enjoy your favorite teas, snacks, and desserts.

Our Story

We started with a love for chai and biscuits, wanting to create a place where you can relax, unwind, and indulge in the perfect combination of flavors.

Our Vision

Our vision is to bring people together over the warmth of a cup of chai and the comfort of a good biscuit. Whether you're here for a quick break or a long catch-up, we strive to create an inviting, friendly atmosphere for all our guests.
